Served as a teaching assistant for the undergraduate course *Fluid Mechanics* at Sichuan University. The course covers the fundamental principles governing fluid motion, including fluid statics, conservation laws, and the relationships among pressure, velocity, and momentum.

My teaching responsibilities included:

- assisting students with fluid statics, continuity, Bernoulli-equation, and momentum-balance problems
- helping students determine appropriate control volumes and apply conservation principles to engineering problems
- explaining the physical meaning behind equations and connecting mathematical solutions to fluid behavior
- supporting tutorial and problem-solving sessions
- answering questions on course content, homework, and reports
- grading assignments and reports and providing feedback on common conceptual errors
