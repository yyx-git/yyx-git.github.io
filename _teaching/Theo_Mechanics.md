Served as a teaching assistant for the undergraduate course *Theoretical Mechanics* at Sichuan University. The course develops the fundamental principles of statics, kinematics, and dynamics and trains students to translate physical problems into mathematical models.

My teaching responsibilities included:

- assisting students with problems in statics, particle motion, and rigid-body dynamics
- helping students identify appropriate coordinate systems, constraints, forces, and equations of motion
- explaining the physical reasoning behind mathematical derivations rather than focusing only on final formulas
- guiding students through multi-step mechanics problems during tutorials and discussions
- answering questions on course concepts and homework
- grading assignments and providing feedback on common reasoning and calculation mistakes
