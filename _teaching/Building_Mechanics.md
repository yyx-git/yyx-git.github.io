Served as a teaching assistant for the undergraduate course *Building Mechanics* at Sichuan University. The course introduces fundamental mechanics concepts for the analysis of structural systems, including equilibrium, internal forces, and the mechanical behavior of basic structural members.

My teaching responsibilities included:

- guiding students through free-body diagrams, support reactions, and equilibrium analysis
- assisting with the analysis of beams, trusses, and other basic structural systems
- helping students understand internal-force diagrams and the physical interpretation of structural responses
- answering questions during tutorials and providing step-by-step explanations for problem sets
- grading assignments and identifying common conceptual and calculation errors
- supporting classroom discussions and course organization
