I’m Yaxuan Yang, a Master’s student in the Department of Mechanics at Sichuan University.

My research lies at the intersection of tropical cyclone dynamics, air–sea interaction, and geophysical fluid dynamics. I am particularly interested in how extreme weather events interact with the Earth system, from landfall processes and Earth’s rotation to the possibility of mitigating typhoon intensity with physics-guided AI.

I am currently working on two related topics centered on landfalling tropical cyclones:
- **Landfalling tropical cyclones perturb Earth’s rotation**
- **Reinforcement learning for pre-landfall typhoon intensity control**

As a researcher, I am drawn to questions that seem counterintuitive at first but turn out to reveal something physically meaningful. I value curiosity, persistence, and physical understanding, and I enjoy combining theory, numerical modeling, and data analysis to study complex geophysical problems. I hope my research can contribute both to fundamental science and to practical disaster mitigation.

### Research interests
- Tropical cyclone dynamics and landfall processes
- Air–sea interaction
- Earth rotation and atmosphere–solid Earth coupling
- Reinforcement learning for geophysical problems

### Methods
- Numerical modeling with CM1
- Reanalysis-based diagnosis
- MATLAB data analysis
- Physics-guided machine learning
